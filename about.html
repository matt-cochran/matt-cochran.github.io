About Matt Cochran

Product and tech leader · Scale Readiness Validator

Matt Cochran is Co-Founder at OutboundLabs. He has over twenty-five years of experience across digital product development: strategic analysis, product and technical leadership, architecture, and operations.

His work focuses on helping Series A SaaS companies validate scale readiness—across architecture and market focus—before they commit more headcount and budget. Previously Founder, CTO at TruthShield; former CTO at Artnet; senior leadership roles at Medidata, Shutterstock, and others. He has led product and engineering teams through legacy modernization, cloud migration, and process and architecture improvements with measurable impact on velocity and deployment.
