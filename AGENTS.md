# Article preparation

- Follow docs/blog-publishing.md and docs/editorial-banners.md for blog work.
- Create new article drafts with npm run article:new so the decorative SVG and frontmatter are prepared together. Choose branches, grid, handoffs, or layers to fit the article problem.
- Preserve reviewed artwork. Never regenerate banners at build or deploy time. Keep SVGs local, decorative, and free of scripts, external assets, and text.
- Keep one reader problem and one concrete example in each article; verify claims against sources and apply the owner’s editorial guide and anti-slop review before publication.
- Creating files or passing a build does not approve or publish article copy. Keep drafts marked draft: true until reviewed.
