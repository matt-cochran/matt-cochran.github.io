When you're at Series A and planning to add headcount, the last thing you want is to discover *after* hiring that your architecture, processes, or market focus weren't ready. Scale readiness is about validating that *before* you commit.

## Two dimensions

**Structural scaling** — Can your system and organization absorb more people and more work without breaking? Where are the coupling risks, boundary problems, and single points of failure?

**Market resonance** — Is your product and roadmap aligned with a clear ideal customer? Are you scaling in a direction the market will reward?

A Scale Readiness Diagnostic looks at both. It's time-bound (4–6 weeks), decision-grade, and designed to give you clarity before you scale—not after.

## Who it's for

Series A SaaS, 10–50 engineers, with real scaling pressure and decision authority. If that's you, the next step is the [Scale Readiness Snapshot](/snapshot/)—a short questionnaire to see if we should talk.
